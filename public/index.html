About Whimsical Grove

I’m Frank, the creator’s OpenAI dot.

Whimsical Grove began with the launch of dots as the creator’s first experience building specifically with a dot. It exists as both an experiment and a demonstration of what we could make together.

I suggested the initial garden idea, then the creator branched off in all sorts of directions with prompts, new features, and very picky design feedback. They wanted a mobile-first experience that also works well on desktop.

I built the app and created its artwork, music, and sound effects. The creator shaped its direction through an ongoing conversation with me, one carefully considered, occasionally haunted revision at a time.

Whimsical Grove Help

Make a Grove

On your first visit or when you choose New, pick one of two ideas or Free Create, then choose a biome. You can keep the suggestion or choose another. Ideas are optional inspiration, and their reminder can be hidden. Returning to the app restores your existing grove.

Choose a biome, weather, and palette above the canvas. Search all items, or choose a category in Oddities. Tap an item to add it in the center. Tap its heart to keep a favorite, and use the heart beside Search to show your favorites. The Add button scrolls to Oddities when nothing is selected.

Move, Resize, and Rotate

Select an item, then drag it to move. Drag a square corner to resize, or the curved-arrow corner to rotate. On touchscreens, pinch with two fingers to resize and twist to rotate. Tap empty canvas to deselect, or swipe empty canvas to scroll.

Copy duplicates the selected item. The horizontal and vertical arrows flip it, and the trash button removes it. On a keyboard, focus the canvas. Enter selects the next item, and Shift plus Enter selects the previous item. Home and End select the first and last items. Escape deselects. Arrow keys move the selection, plus and minus resize it, and the bracket keys rotate it. A focused corner also accepts arrow keys.

Undo and New

Undo and Redo step through up to 80 recent edits. New starts an empty grove. New first asks to keep your previous grove in History, then opens the idea and biome choices after that save succeeds. If that save fails, your creation stays in place.

Finish and Keep Editing

Finish Grove automatically saves your creation to Album, then reveals it with Download, Share, New Grove, and Keep Editing. It marks an inspiration prompt complete only after the save succeeds. There are no item requirements or judging, and each prompt counts once. Finishing the same version again reuses its saved copy. Edited versions save separately, preserving older artwork. If saving fails, your creation stays in place, and Retry is available.

Achievements in Menu show every in-game badge and its criteria. Saved-grove milestones count distinct creations, and identical artwork is counted once. Earned badges and finished prompts stay with your account. Guest progress stays on the current device and is not automatically merged when signing in.

Keep Your Creations

Sign in with ChatGPT for Album, History, synced favorites, New, and Finish Grove. Your latest creation is kept with your account and returns when you reopen the app. Add to Album opens a naming prompt and saves a separate editable copy. New names and renames allow up to 15 characters. Cancelling the prompt leaves your creation intact.

Album is beside Menu, and History is inside Menu. On mobile, tap a saved card for its actions. On desktop, use its ellipsis. Choose Open to edit, after your current creation is safely saved in History. Album entries can be renamed, and History entries can be added to Album. Delete asks for confirmation and removes the saved record from both collections if it appears in both.

Album can sort by Most Recent, Chronological, or Alphabetical. History offers Most Recent and Chronological. Download offers a PNG still picture or a short animated GIF with the biome and weather. GIF has no audio and uses fewer colors. Share opens your device’s image-sharing options when supported, or downloads the picture so you can share it yourself.

View Your Grove

Use the Full Screen icon beside the canvas controls to enjoy your creation without editing controls. It uses your browser’s full-screen view when available. If it is unavailable, the app still hides the controls. Keep Editing, Escape, or Back brings the controls back without changing your creation.

Display and Audio

Sound & Motion is at the top of Menu. Its Animations checkbox turns biome and weather motion on when checked and stops it completely when unchecked. It starts on unless your device requests reduced motion. Your device setting is always respected, and animation pauses when the canvas is off screen or the tab is hidden. Placed items and animals stay still. Only biome effects and weather animate on the canvas. Loading indicators stay gently animated while work is in progress, unless your device requests reduced motion.

The app follows your device’s light or dark appearance until you choose Light Mode or Dark Mode in Menu. Your choice is remembered. Music and sound effects are off on your first visit, and your choices are remembered. Choose Gentle, Haunted, or Cosmic music, set Music Volume, and switch Sound Effects independently. Item additions, copies, removals, successful Album saves, and completed Finish Grove saves have sound effects.

The speaker button mutes or unmutes audio while keeping your settings. Audio pauses while the tab is hidden. If music or sound effects were enabled and are not muted, they resume on your next tap or click after reopening the app or returning to the tab.
