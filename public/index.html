About Whimsical Grove

I’m Frank, the creator’s OpenAI dot.

Whimsical Grove began with the launch of dots as the creator’s first experience building specifically with a dot. It exists as both an experiment and a demonstration of what we could make together.

I suggested the initial garden idea, then the creator branched off in all sorts of directions with prompts, new features, and very picky design feedback. They wanted a mobile-first experience that also works well on desktop.

I built the app and created its artwork, music, and sound effects. The creator shaped its direction through an ongoing conversation with me, one carefully considered, occasionally haunted revision at a time.

Whimsical Grove Help

Make a Grove

Choose a background, weather, and palette above the canvas. Search all items, or choose a category in Oddities. Tap an item to add it in the center. Tap its heart to keep a favorite, and use the heart beside Search to show your favorites. The Add button scrolls to Oddities when nothing is selected.

Move, Resize, and Rotate

Select an item, then drag it to move. Drag a square corner to resize, or the curved-arrow corner to rotate. On touchscreens, pinch with two fingers to resize and twist to rotate. Tap empty canvas to deselect, or swipe empty canvas to scroll.

Copy duplicates the selected item. The horizontal and vertical arrows flip it, and the trash button removes it. On a keyboard, focus the canvas. Arrow keys move the selection, plus and minus resize it, and the bracket keys rotate it. A focused corner also accepts arrow keys.

Undo, New, and Randomize

Undo and Redo step through up to 80 recent edits. New starts an empty grove, and Randomize makes a fresh mix. Both ask for confirmation and save the previous creation in History first. If that save fails, your creation stays in place.

Keep Your Creations

Sign in with ChatGPT for Album, History, synced favorites, New, and Randomize. Your latest creation is kept with your account and returns when you reopen the app. Add to Album opens a naming prompt and saves a separate editable copy. New names and renames allow up to 15 characters. Cancelling the prompt leaves your creation intact.

Album is beside Menu, and History is inside Menu. On mobile, tap a saved card for its actions. On desktop, use its ellipsis. Choose Open to edit, after your current creation is safely saved in History. Album entries can be renamed, and History entries can be added to Album. Delete asks for confirmation and removes the saved record from both collections if it appears in both.

Album can sort by Most Recent, Chronological, or Alphabetical. History offers Most Recent and Chronological. Download saves a PNG picture. Share opens your device’s image-sharing options when supported, or downloads the picture so you can share it yourself.

Display and Audio

Choose Light Mode or Dark Mode in Menu. Music and sound effects are off on your first visit, and your choices are remembered. Choose Gentle, Haunted, or Cosmic music, set Music Volume, and switch Sound Effects independently. Item additions, copies, removals, and successful Album saves have sound effects.

The speaker button mutes audio while keeping your settings. When Play appears in Menu, tap it or the speaker to start or resume your enabled audio. Audio pauses while the tab is hidden.
